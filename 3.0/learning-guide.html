Take the role of a staff member at Bagwan. Use the records, services and people directory to respond to a workplace request.

Follow an interpreter request

You’re covering reception. Alex Morgan asks whether an interpreter has been booked for an upcoming appointment. Prepare a handover for the next staff member.

  1. Read Alex Morgan’s record →

    Note what Alex asked for and what the record confirms.

  2. Visit the three stops

    At main reception, interpreting reception and the nurses’ station, find the service that could help. Consider what you could say at a public desk and what you would discuss privately.

    Start the three-stop orientation → Find interpreting services

  3. Write your handover

    Write two sentences for your colleague, followed by one question you would ask Alex.

Check your handover

Request and booking status
Have you named Alex’s request and accurately described the recorded booking status?
Source and next service
Have you identified where the information came from and which service to contact next?
Question for Alex
Does your question clarify a missing detail without assuming Alex’s preferred language or dialect?

Other workplace tasks

  • Find a policy →

    Choose a workplace question with a peer. Record the policy title and the section that supports your answer.

  • Find a colleague →

    Use the people directory to find a role that could respond. Give the department and one reason for your choice.

  • Record an observation →

    Choose a clinical form for your assigned patient. Enter the available observations, identify any missing information and check your entries against the record.

Optional practice

  • PPE selection decisions →

    Explain which information supports a decision about protective equipment.

  • Waste classification examples →

    Identify what determines each classification and when you need more information.

  • Finding an interpreter →

    Use a separate appointment scenario to practise searching the NAATI directory and recording your reasoning.

Facilitator debrief

Exchange handovers

Ask pairs to swap handovers. Each listener points to the record entry that supports the booking status, identifies who could follow up and asks how the writer chose their question for Alex.

Change one detail

If Alex later confirms a preferred language and dialect, which line of the handover changes? Which detail still needs confirmation from the booking service?
