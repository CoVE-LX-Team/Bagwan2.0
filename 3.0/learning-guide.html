BAGWAN HEALTH

Learning guide

Take the role of a staff member at Bagwan. Use the records, services and people directory to respond to a workplace request.

Follow an interpreter request

Alex Morgan asks whether an interpreter has been booked for an upcoming appointment. You are covering reception. Prepare a handover so the next staff member can follow up.

  1. Read Alex Morgan’s record. Note what Alex asked for and what the record confirms.
  2. Visit main reception, interpreting reception and the nurses’ station. Find the service that could help. Consider what you could say at a public desk and what you would discuss privately.
  3. Write a two-sentence handover to your colleague, followed by one question you would ask Alex.

Check your handover

  • It names Alex’s request and accurately describes the recorded booking status.
  • It identifies the source of the information and a service to contact next.
  • Your question addresses an outstanding detail without assuming Alex’s preferred language or dialect.

Other workplace tasks

Find a policy
Choose a workplace question with a peer. Find a relevant policy or procedure, record its title and point to the section that supports your answer.
Find a colleague
Use the people directory to identify the role that could respond to your question. Give the department and one reason for your choice.
Record an observation
Use the details supplied for your assigned patient. Choose a clinical form, record the available observations and identify any information you still need. Compare your entries with the source record.

Optional practice

  • PPE selection decisions

    Explain which information supports a decision about protective equipment.

  • Waste classification examples

    Identify the detail that determines each classification and when you need more information.

  • Finding an interpreter

    Use a separate appointment scenario to practise searching the NAATI directory and recording your reasoning.

Facilitator debrief

Ask pairs to exchange handovers. Have each listener point to the record entry that supports the booking status, identify who could follow up and ask the writer how they chose their question for Alex.

Change one condition for discussion. If Alex later confirms a preferred language and dialect, which line of the handover changes? Which detail still requires confirmation from the booking service?
