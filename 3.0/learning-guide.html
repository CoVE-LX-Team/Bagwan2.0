An interpreter for Alex’s appointment

You’re at main reception. Alex Morgan wants to know whether an interpreter will be there for an upcoming appointment. Your shift is nearly over.

  1. Read Alex Morgan’s record →

    Is there a booking confirmation in the reception note?

  2. Find out who can help

    Follow the request from the public desk to interpreting reception and the nurses’ station. If you need to ask Alex about their appointment, where could you speak privately?

    Start the three-stop orientation → Find interpreting services

  3. Hand over to the next shift

    Your colleague is taking over at reception. Leave them a note with what Alex asked for, what you found in the record and what still needs following up.

Facilitator debrief

Take the next shift

Swap notes with a colleague. Could you pick up the enquiry from their note? Tell them what you’d do next and what, if anything, you’d need to ask.

Example handover note

Alex Morgan asked whether an interpreter is booked for their appointment. I couldn’t find a confirmation in the record. Please check Alex’s preferred language and dialect with them, follow up with interpreting services and let Alex know.

When Alex calls back

Alex calls back to confirm their preferred language and dialect. What can reception now record? What do you still need from interpreting services before you can give Alex an answer?
