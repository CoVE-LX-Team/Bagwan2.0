LEARNING GUIDE · BAGWAN 3.0

Learn through the workplace.

Use Bagwan as a hospital staff member would. Keep the learning task, reflection and evidence here, separate from the intranet.

For facilitator review
These suggested activities and new safety references await SME and SLD review. They are not approved assessment instruments or clinical instructions.

Before you begin

Confirm your role, the scenario and the evidence your facilitator expects. Use the supplied fictional patient information. Browser drafts are stored on your device; they are not submitted to an LMS.

Suggested workplace tasks

  1. Find the right information. Locate a policy, identify its scope and describe how it applies to your assigned role. Distinguish migrated source policies from unapproved drafts.
  2. Find the right person. Use the people directory and reporting structures to identify who could help with a workplace query. Explain your reasoning.
  3. Use a patient record. Open a supplied patient scenario and complete the relevant observation form with the scenario information. Review the record before downloading or printing your draft.
  4. Communicate clearly. Explore the interpreting scenario and discuss the effect of the decisions made. Use the current NAATI directory when investigating practitioner credentials.

Your facilitator should map selected tasks to the unit requirements, learner level and evidence criteria before use. No curriculum mapping or competency judgement is implied by this preview.

Reflect and debrief

What information supported your decision? What was missing? What would you clarify with a colleague or supervisor? What evidence would demonstrate your reasoning?

The practical conversation, feedback and evidence belong in your teaching and assessment workflow. Bagwan supplies the workplace context.
