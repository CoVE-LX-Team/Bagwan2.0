D01Medication safetyReview status: Pending

Why this was created
No source policy was supplied
Reviewers
  • Clinical SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Purpose and scope

Use this reference when checking medication information and handing responsibility to another member of the care team.

Check the record

  • Confirm the patient’s identity using the local identification procedure.
  • Check the current medication list, allergies and documented adverse reactions.
  • If information is missing or conflicts with another record, ask the supervising clinician or pharmacist to reconcile it before relying on it.

Communicate changes

Record discrepancies, who was contacted and the agreed follow-up. At a transition of care, pass the current list and outstanding questions to the receiving clinician. Use the incident reporting process for a medication incident or near miss.

D02Nursing and clinical handoverReview status: Pending

Why this was created
No source policy was supplied
Reviewers
  • Nursing SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Prepare the handover

Identify the patient using the local identification procedure. Check the record, ask about the patient’s priorities and gather the latest relevant observations.

Give the handover

  1. Introduce yourself and identify the colleague receiving the handover.
  2. Describe the current situation, relevant background and changes since the last handover. Distinguish observations from information that still needs checking.
  3. Explain each outstanding action, who is responsible and when it needs attention.

Close the loop

Invite questions. Ask the receiving colleague to confirm the next action and who will take it. Document the transfer of responsibility and any unresolved concerns in the patient record.

D03Booking an interpreterReview status: Pending

Why this was created
The original page contained an unfilled policy template
Reviewers
  • Interpreting SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Ask about communication needs

Ask the person which language and dialect they prefer and whether they have a preference relevant to the booking. Record their answer and the support requested.

Prepare the booking

Gather the appointment date, time, duration, location or remote format, and purpose. Use the language service’s booking channel. Share only the details needed to arrange the service.

Track the request

Record when the request was made, who will follow up and which details are outstanding. Once the service confirms the booking, record the confirmation and tell the person and appointment team.

Before the appointment

Check the room or remote connection, allow time for briefing, and introduce the interpreter and their role. Speak directly to the person and allow time for questions.

D07Laboratory waste referenceReview status: Pending

Why this was created
The archived answer key categorised unspecified expired medication as cytotoxic waste
Reviewers
  • Laboratory safety and waste SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Identify the material

Check what the material is, what it has been used for and what it may be contaminated with. Use the local waste procedure and the label on the designated container to confirm the waste stream.

If the waste stream is unclear

Leave the item where it can be managed safely and ask the supervisor how to proceed. The appearance of an item or the colour of a container does not establish the correct waste stream. For an expired medicine, identify the medicine before deciding how it should be managed.
