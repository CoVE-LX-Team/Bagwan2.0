D01Medication safetyReview status: Pending

Why this was created
No source policy was supplied
Reviewers
  • Clinical SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Purpose and scope

This draft describes medication safety responsibilities for the Bagwan simulation. It applies to the use of medication information in learning scenarios. It does not authorise prescribing, dispensing or administration.

Before proceeding

  • Work within your role, competence and the approved local procedure.
  • Confirm the patient identity and the current medication information provided in the scenario.
  • Check the documented allergy and adverse drug reaction history.
  • Refer discrepancies, missing information or uncertainty to the supervising clinician or pharmacist before proceeding.

Documentation and communication

Keep medication information accurate, communicate changes during transitions of care, and record the relevant observations and actions. Use the incident reporting process for a medication incident or near miss.

Approval requirements

The SME must confirm responsibilities, local escalation arrangements, required checks and documentation before this draft is used for teaching. No medicine, dose, administration instruction or checking protocol is specified here.

D02Nursing and clinical handoverReview status: Pending

Why this was created
No source policy was supplied
Reviewers
  • Nursing SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Purpose

Support a structured transfer of relevant information and responsibility during the Bagwan simulation.

Prepare the handover

Use the handover structure approved for the learning scenario. Check the patient identity against the scenario record. Gather the current situation, relevant background, observations, identified risks, pending actions and the patient's preferences.

Give and receive the handover

  • Identify who is handing over and who will accept responsibility.
  • Communicate accurate, relevant and current information in a logical order.
  • State outstanding actions, who will complete them and when they require attention.
  • Allow questions and confirm shared understanding.
  • Record the handover and clarify any information that remains uncertain.

Approval requirements

The SME must define the required identifiers, handover framework, minimum information and escalation process for each scenario. This draft is a communication framework, not an approved clinical protocol.

D03Booking an interpreterReview status: Pending

Why this was created
The original page contained an unfilled policy template
Reviewers
  • Interpreting SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Prepare the request

Confirm the person's preferred language, dialect and communication needs. Identify the appointment time, expected duration, location or remote format, and the purpose of the appointment. Record any preferences or circumstances relevant to selecting an interpreter.

Arrange the service

Use the organisation's approved language service arrangements. Confirm the interpreter's suitability and credentials for the task. Provide the information required for the booking through the approved channel and confirm the arrangements with the patient and the appointment team.

Before the appointment

Plan suitable space, equipment and time. Brief the interpreter on the purpose and relevant context. Check that the patient understands the interpreter's role.

In this simulation

No external booking is sent. Record a proposed booking in your learning evidence and discuss it with your facilitator. The SME must confirm provider arrangements, urgency pathways and documentation requirements before approval.

D06Laboratory PPE referenceReview status: Pending

Why this was created
Replace the archived drag activity with an appropriate intranet reference and separate practice
Reviewers
  • Laboratory safety SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Use the task risk assessment

Select protective equipment using the approved procedure and the hazards of the task. The original activity's single set of clothing choices does not establish suitable PPE for every laboratory task.

Before work

  • Confirm the task, hazards and approved controls.
  • Check the specified protective clothing, hand protection, eye protection and footwear.
  • Check that equipment fits, is in good condition and is suitable for the task.
  • Clarify any missing instruction with the supervisor before proceeding.

Practical skills

Selection, fit, use and removal require supervised practice. The optional HTML exercise is in the learning guide and does not assess practical competence.

D07Laboratory waste referenceReview status: Pending

Why this was created
The archived answer key categorised unspecified expired medication as cytotoxic waste
Reviewers
  • Laboratory safety and waste SME
  • Andrew — Senior Learning Designer

Identify the waste before disposal

Use the current local waste procedure and the labels on the approved containers. Waste classification depends on what the material is and what it is contaminated with.

When information is missing

Do not guess from a picture or container colour. Ask the supervisor to confirm the classification and handling requirements.

Archived activity correction

The original activity described expired medication without specifying its type. That description does not establish that the medication is cytotoxic. The draft practice exercise uses an explicitly identified cytotoxic residue instead. The SME must review every category and example before use.
