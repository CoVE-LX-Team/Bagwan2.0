Working with allied health

20 November 2026

Start with the person’s goals

A colleague has asked about support after discharge. What does the person want to do at home? What support do they already have? Use their answers to describe the need in their own terms.

Find the relevant discipline

The Victorian Department of Health describes professions including occupational therapy, physiotherapy, speech pathology, dietetics and social work. Use its directory to look up a discipline and discuss its possible contribution with the person and their care team.

Victorian Department of Health · Allied health professions

Prepare a team discussion

  • Record the person’s goal and the question they want answered.
  • Clarify what is already documented and which details still need confirmation.
  • Ask the supervising team who will follow up and how the person would like to be involved.

From a goal to a discussion

A patient says, “I want to be able to cook for myself when I get home.” Ask which part of preparing a meal feels difficult. Bring their answer to the care team when discussing the support they would find useful.
