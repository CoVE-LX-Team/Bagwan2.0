Offer a choice

Give the person an opportunity to say whether they would like liaison support. Ask who they would like involved in the conversation and what, if anything, they are comfortable sharing. Respect their preferences, including a decision to decline.

One way to offer support is to ask, “Would you like to speak with a liaison officer? Is there anyone you’d like included in that conversation?”

Understand the liaison role

The Royal Melbourne Hospital’s First Nations Health Unit describes how its liaison officers support Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander patients and families during a hospital stay. Read its description as an example of the work of a liaison service.
