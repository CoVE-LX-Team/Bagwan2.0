Interpreting interactive professional development activity

In conjunction with some of the interpreting team, Bagwan Health have scripted, filmed and created an excellent short professional development activity. All staff are welcome to complete this to work through some crucial decision points that interpreters face.

The following professional development activity is an excellent introduction to when interpreters face crucial ethical and professional decision points and how they can best deal with these.

Select ‘Start scenario’ to begin the professional development activity. By the end of this module you will be able to clearly identify several key decision points when interpreting for clients in a health context.

Branching scenario

Objective

Simulated scenarios for the user to practice knowledge and skills. Scenarios relate to best practice, ethics, and code of conduct related to accredited interpreter. The activity will have question and decision points along the way using film/videos of scenarios and decision outcomes.

Context for scenarios

Consultation with specialist doctor – Cardiologist to discuss medical results. Dialogue to revolve around medical results for Patient. Doctor diagnoses high-cholesterol based on latest blood test, and advises Patient how to manage high blood pressure better.

Characters in scenario include:

Interpreter Cardiologist Mrs Trang (patient) Parker (grandson)

Introduction 

This activity promotes best practice on how to establish a interpreter-client relationship. 

You will assume the role of a an interpreter. You’re assigned to interpret a medical consultation between doctor and patient.

Based on code of conduct related dilemmas, you will face decision points on how to proceed along the way.

Please note: The scenarios presented are a fictional account to represent ethical issues. Any resemblance to actual persons is unintentional.

 

  • Next

How it works

  • Watch interactions between client, community doctor, and certified interpreter.
  • After each interaction, you are to select the most appropriate response. Possible response outcomes can be either : A good approach or not the best approach
  • You can redo and repeat this activity many times to practice and to improve your responses.
  • This activity has sound, so please adjust your volume as needed.

This activity uses high resolution videos for each scenario. For best results, please use high-speed Internet connectivity.

How do you proceed?

  • B. Family member can interpret for Patient during consultation.

Video outcome A

An interpretor’s role is to help improve communication, and clinical outcomes, providing satisfaction with care in patients with limited English proficiency.

Video outcomes B

 

Not the best result

If family members, friends or staff who are not trained as accredited interpreters try to interpret, risks are that errors in understanding and communication are more likely to occur. This can pose grave risks to the patient as well as liability to the healthcare provider or institution.
