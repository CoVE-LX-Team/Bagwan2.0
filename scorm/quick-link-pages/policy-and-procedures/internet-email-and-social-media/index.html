Internet, email and social media

Policy Name – Internet, Email and Social Media
Policy Number
Version Number
Scope

This policy applies to all Bagwan Health, staff, contractors, Allied Health professionals, Credentialed Specialists, volunteers and students.

Policy

This policy defines the standards and processes associated with the responsible use of internet, email and social media at Bagwan Health. This policy has been developed in conjunction with the National Code of Conduct for Health Care Workers and other relevant VIC Health and Bagwan Health policies and legislation.

Procedure 

Internet, Email and Social Media

Internet, email and social media are digital methods of communication and allow individuals and organization to communicate, find and share information, distribute resources and much more. Access to the internet at Bagwan Health is a privilege and everyone must adhere to this policy.

Internet and Email Usage

Bagwan Health staff, contractors, volunteers and students are expected to use the internet responsibly and productively. Internet access is limited to job-related activities only and personal use is not permitted. Job-related activities include research and educational tasks that can be completed using the internet that assist a person in their role.

All internet data that is composed, transmitted and/or received by Bagwan Health computer systems is considered to belong to Bagwan Health and is recognized as part of its official data. It is therefore subject to disclosure for legal reasons or to other appropriate third parties.

The equipment, services and technology used to access the internet are the property of Bagwan Health, and as such Bagwan Health reserves the right to monitor internet traffic and monitor and access data that is composed, sent or received through its online connections.

Emails sent via the company email system should not contain content that is deemed to be offensive. This includes, though is not restricted to, the use of vulgar or harassing language/images.

All sites and downloads may be monitored and/or blocked by Bagwan Health if they are deemed to be harmful and/or not productive to business.

Installing Software

The installation of new software such as internet browsers or instant messaging technology is strictly prohibited. If there is a requirement for new software to be installed, a request must be made via the I.T. portal on the intranet home page. All requests must be approved prior to installation by the relevant manager and the I.T. Manager.

 

What is Unacceptable Use of Internet and Email?

 

Unacceptable use of the internet or email includes, but is not limited to:

·         Sending or posting discriminatory, harassing, or threatening messages or images on the internet or via Bagwan Health’s email service

·         Using computers to perpetrate fraud, and/or software, film or music piracy

·         Stealing, using, or disclosing someone else’s password without authorization

·         Downloading, copying or pirating software and electronic files that are copyrighted or without authorization

·         Sharing confidential material or proprietary information outside of the organization

·         Hacking into unauthorized websites

·         Sending or posting information that is defamatory to the company, its products/services, colleagues and/or customers

·         Introducing malicious software onto the company network and/or jeopardizing the security of the organization’s electronic communications systems

·         Sending or posting chain letters, solicitations, or advertisements not related to business purposes or activities

 

·         Passing off personal views as representing those of Bagwan Health

If unsure about what constitutes acceptable internet usage, then consult a manager for further guidance and clarification

Social Media

Social media is a popular communication method used to interact, share content and collaborate outside of traditional means of communication. The growing use of social media and networking sites is providing opportunities for Bagwan Health to communicate and share information. Social media is also a powerful engagement tool for Bagwan Health to communicate with staff, external stakeholders and the community. 

For the purpose of this policy, social media includes, but is not limited to:

·         Social sites/apps e.g. Facebook or Twitter

·         Business networking sites/apps e.g. LinkedIn

·         Video sharing sites/apps e.g. YouTube

·         Photo sharing sites/apps e.g. Instagram

·         Blogs e.g. WordPress

·         Forums and discussion boards e.g. Yahoo! or Google Groups

·         Podcasts e.g. Simplecast

 

·         Any other websites that allow individual users or companies to use simple publishing tools

Always remember that posts and comments made on social media, either professionally or personally, could potentially be permanent.

Bagwan Health’s Use of Social Media

Bagwan Health uses social media for the following purposes:

·         Encourage feedback from the public

·         Engage with members of the community and community groups

·         Facilitate a communication channel for the public to access information about our services

·         Facilitate a communication channel for health promotion

·         Promote Bagwan Health events

·         Facilitate a communication for public health announcements such as new programs, outbreaks

·         Provide interesting and useful information for the community

·         Engage a younger demographic

 

·         Enable information sharing for staff, contractors, volunteers, students and clinicians

Inappropriate Material for Social Media

Social media can be a fun personal platform, but it can create problems professionally, especially when inappropriate material is shared. Inappropriate material includes, but is not limited to, any material that is:

 

·         Misleading or deceiving, whether intended or not

·         Offensive, threatening, intimidating, abusive, defamatory or that may lead to criminal or civil liability

·         Discriminating against colleagues, patients/clients or the public, on the grounds of sex, pregnancy, marital status, age, race, nationality, cultural or ethnic background, religious background, disability, HIV/AIDS, sexuality

·         Victimising, harassing, vilifying or bullying

·         Potentially damaging to Bagwan Health or Bagwan Health’s reputation in the community

Information that is retained and secured by Bagwan Health, in accordance with Section 5 of the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 or the Health Services Act 1997, is also inappropriate for sharing on social media.

 

This includes private health information, records, photos or images, tax file numbers, employee personal details. 

Responsibilities

All staff, contractors, volunteers and students have a responsibility to:

·         Act in accordance with this policy and any other relevant procedures and legislation when using social media

·         Report any instances of inappropriate material, comments or intellectual property being posted to their line manager or a more senior staff member

·         Carry out their duties responsibly, effectively, efficiently and in accordance to the National Code of Conduct for Health Care Workers

·         Ensure they only access and use social media sites outside working hours in a private capacity, unless for the purpose of carrying out their duties

·         Use Bagwan Health computers and network connections appropriately and in line with relevant policies.

Managers are to:

·         Ensure that staff, contractors, volunteers and students working within their area of management understand this policy

 

·         Investigate any alleged breach of the National Code of Conduct for Health Care Workers by their staff

Private Use of Social Media

Staff, contractors, volunteers and students should not expect to be able to access and use social media during working hours, unless for the purpose of carrying out their duties. The use of social for personal purposes while at work is strictly prohibited.

Outside of work hours, they should be reminded that anything they post to their personal accounts can be potentially seen by anyone with access to social media.

Staff, contractors, volunteers and students with personal social media accounts should be reminded that:

·         Anything they post on their personal accounts can potentially be seen by anyone with access to social media

·         It is their responsibility to know who their online friends/contacts are and to use privacy settings accordingly

·         Identifying or discussing their place of work in their personal comments is not allowed

·         They should not accept friend requests from former or existing patients as this can breach professional boundaries

·         Making comments or remarks about patients that have been treated at Bagwan Health may breach the Code of Conduct and break patient confidentiality

·         Inappropriate material, which may contravene any Victorian Health Policy or bring VIC Health or Bagwan Health into disrepute, should not be uploaded to personal accounts

·         They must not offer opinions or comments on behalf of Bagwan Health or imply that their comments are made as a representative of Bagwan Health, as to do so would breach the Code of Conduct. 

Violation of Internet, Email and Social Media Use Policy

 

Violation of this policy could result in disciplinary and/or legal action leading up to and including termination of employment. Staff, contractors, volunteers and students may also be held personally liable for damages caused by any violations of this policy.

Bagwan Health on Social Media

Bagwan Health social media pages, profiles and sites are developed to engage with the public. The content of the social media is intended for public use.

Any Bagwan staff member, contractor, volunteer or student who wishes to create social media sites or accounts representing Bagwan Health or any of its facilities or services must first obtain permission from their Director, followed by the Media and Communications Manager.  

 

The content on Bagwan Health social media pages, profiles and sites will be captured and archived on a regular basis in accordance with State Records Guideline – Records Management and Web 2.0.
