OHS/WHS

Policy Name

Workplace Occupational Health and Safety

Policy Number

002

Owner of Policy

Dr Christina Jones, CEO

Scope

The primary focus of all activities within Bagwan Health will be the provision of excellence in service user services whilst equally maintaining a position of excellence in the management of Workplace Occupational Health and Safety.

The goal for Bagwan Health in Health and Safety is the pro-active prevention of work-related accidents, incidents, injuries and illnesses and the development and promotion of safe systems of work. Committed to ensuring, as far as is reasonably practicable, the health, safety and welfare of the working environment for its staff, volunteers, contractors and visitors to its premises.

This policy applies to all Bagwan Health employees, contractors, volunteers and visitors to Bagwan Health’s premises, to the extent it is relevant to them.

In this policy, ‘workplace’ includes working on site or off-site, attendance at a work-related conference or function, and attendance at a client or other work-related event, including retreats and social events.

 

Term and definitions

 

Authorised person

An authorised person is the relevant Manager or the Chief Executive Officer.

 

Employee

An employee is a Bagwan Health employee, volunteer or student on placement with Bagwan Health.

 

Individual

Individual refers to Bagwan Health service users, families and friends, employees, potential employees, volunteers, students and the community.

 

Service user

A service user is a child or an adult (person with a disability) or aged person who uses a Bagwan Health service.

 

Workplace

Workplace includes working on-site or off-site, attendance at a work-related conference or function, and attendance at a client or other work-related event, including retreats and social events.

 
Policy

Bagwan Health supports the rights of all persons covered by the policy to work in an environment that is, so far as is reasonably practicable, safe and without risks to health.

Bagwan Health is committed to the promotion of a joint and united approach to consultation and resolution of Work Health and Safety issues, with a view to improving workplace efficiency and productivity.

This will be accomplished through the ongoing development, in consultation with Bagwan Health’s Safety Representatives, of management systems and procedures designed to, so far as is reasonably practicable:

• identify, assess and control workplace hazards;

• reduce the incidence and cost of occupational injury and illness; and

• provide a rehabilitation system for those affected by occupational injury or illness.

Workplace Occupational Health and Safety statutory requirements, including regulations and codes of practice, are minimum standards and so the aim is for them to be improved upon, where practicable.

 

Procedures

Bagwan Health follows legislated best practice standards to help create a safe workplace. Bagwan Health refers to detailed Workplace Occupational Health and Safety procedures as set out by Worksafe Victoria’s Health and Safety guides:

Health and Safety Committee

The Bagwan Health Workplace Occupational Health and Safety Committee should meet at least every three months in a year to monitor and facilitate the development and implementation of Health and Safety Systems, throughout the organisation.

Bagwan Health Designated Work Groups are:

• Respite & Recreation

• Therapy & Inclusion Services

• Accommodation & Specialist Support Services

• Child & Youth Services

• Quality, Innovation & Service Development

• People Culture & Resources

• Finance

• Support Coordination.

 

Each Designated Work Group has at least one OH&S Committee member acting as its Health and Safety Representative. The Program Manager for each Designated Work area is also required to attend the Bagwan Health OH&S Committee meetings.

Management should review the relevant legislation to determine whether a Health and Safety Committee is required for the workplace. Management should also consult with the Health and Safety Representatives and the Human Resources Department to determine if a Committee is required at their site.

Where a Health and Safety Committee is required by legislation, or where the CEO otherwise deems it necessary, Bagwan Health will establish a Health and Safety Committee.

 

Health and Safety Committee Membership

The number of Committee members will be decided and agreed by management, in consultation with employees.

Elected health and safety representatives may also be Health and Safety Committee members. At least half of the Health and Safety Committee members must be employee representatives.

Health and Safety Committee meetings are to be conducted at least every three months in a year. An agenda will be established prior to the meeting and issued to all persons attending the meeting. Minutes will be taken and forwarded to all Health and Safety Representatives, and copies shall be made available to all employees.

Bagwan Health encourages sites to invite appropriate visitors to attend the Occupational Health and Safety meetings and positively contribute.

 

Health and Safety Representative

Management must ensure that employee representatives are selected appropriately and that the Committee is established and conducted in accordance with the applicable legislation.

The Committee Representatives are required to:

• Undertake relevant training, i.e. OH&S Representative Course, to carry the role out effectively

• Be aware of the Health and Safety interests of their Designated Work Group, and represent their concerns to their relevant Manager and the Occupational Health & Safety Committee

• Attend the OH&S Committee meetings as scheduled.

 

Committee Representatives can conduct Safety Mapping, Site Safety and Vehicle or First Aid Kit Checks and participate in the consultation processes to complete Risk Assessments aiming to best manage safety issues if required.

Where required by law, or where deemed necessary, designated workgroups shall each elect a Health and Safety Representative as their elected spokesperson.

Health and Safety Representatives are encouraged to work with management to discuss Work Health and Safety issues and to work with management to improve health and safety standards.

 

Dissemination of Policy and Procedure

The Workplace Occupational Health & Safety Policy and related procedures will be displayed in the workplace and all employees and volunteers will be provided with a copy by their supervisor/manager.

New employees will be provided with a copy of the documents as part of their induction.

The policy and related procedures will be reviewed on an annual basis or more frequently, if required, to ensure continued compliance with the relevant legislation.

 

Breach of Workplace Occupational Health and Safety Policy

Any breach of this policy may result in counselling and/or disciplinary action, which, in the case of employees, may lead to dismissal, or, in the case of volunteers, may lead to the cessation of their engagement.

Any breach of this policy by a contractor may result in the cancellation by Bagwan Health of the services provided by that contractor.

 

Roles and responsibilities

Bagwan Health acknowledges that the active cooperation of all managers, employees, volunteers’ students and service users is required for ensuring a safe and healthy working environment. Consequently, the management of Bagwan Health is committed to a consultative approach to Workplace Occupational Health and Safety.

All Bagwan Health employees, volunteers and students are expected to contribute to the improvement of health and safety within the workplace and to help facilitate cooperation on health and safety issues and to ensuring health and safety measures are implemented.

 

CEO and other executives

The CEO and other executive officers will:

• be committed to the provision and maintenance of a healthy and safe workplace

• consult and participate in the health and safety program

• use risk identification, assessment and control principles to reach Bagwan Health’s health and safety objectives

• inform and train all persons to whom this procedure applies in relevant policies, procedures and health and safety obligations

• participate in Bagwan Health’s inductions and implement all safety procedures.

 

Human Resources

The Human Resources Department will:

• be committed to the provision and maintenance of a healthy and safe workplace

• ensure that all staff receive the appropriate training in the policy and related procedures, and their obligations under occupational health and safety laws.

 

Supervisors and Managers

Supervisors/managers will:

• be committed to the provision and maintenance of a healthy and safe workplace

• consult and participate in the health and safety program

• use risk identification, assessment and control principles to reach Bagwan Health’s health and safety objectives

• inform and train all staff in relevant policies, procedures and health and safety obligations

• participate in Bagwan Health inductions and implement all safety procedures.

 

Employees

Employees will: 

• participate in health and safety training, actions and activities and support Bagwan Health in its efforts to reach its health and safety and, where relevant, rehabilitation objectives

• follow reasonable health and safety instructions from managers or supervisors;

• report any serious incidents, accidents, injuries or hazards in the workplace to supervisors or designated representatives

• aim to work in a way that does not endanger the health or safety of themselves or others

• properly use and maintain safety equipment

• make sure visitors follow safety rules in the workplace

• participate in Bagwan Health induction programs and implement all detailed safety procedures

• assess risks to their health and safety arising from the provision of their service

• have control measures in place to address those risks, including complying with any relevant Bagwan Health policies and practices.

 

Health and Safety Committee

The role of the Health and Safety Committee is to:

• assist in the development, monitoring and review of health and safety policies and procedures

• consider any proposal for, or changes to the workplace, policies, work practices or procedures which may affect the health and safety of employees

• promote the importance of health and safety amongst management and employees

• monitor Bagwan Health’s health and safety performance

• monitor the rehabilitation of injured employees

• assist in the resolution of health and safety disputes.

The Committee will meet on a quarterly basis, or more frequently if required. An agenda will be circulated by the Head of the Committee prior to the meeting. A sample agenda can be found in Appendix B to this procedure. A designated note-taker will take minutes of the meeting. A sample minutes document can be found in Appendix A to this procedure. 

 

Health and Safety Representatives

The role of Health and Safety Representatives is to:

• represent employees from their workgroup in relation to health and safety matters

• investigate health and safety-related complaints prior to representations to management

• make representations to management and report back to employees on any matter relating to health and safety

• discuss with the employees, any proposals or matters which may affect the health and safety of employees

• assist management in the identification of hazards, the assessment of risks and implementation of risk control measures

• assist in promoting adherence to health and safety policies and procedures

• assist in the monitoring of risk controls and health and safety policies and procedures.
